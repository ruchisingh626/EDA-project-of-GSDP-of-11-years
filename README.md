This Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) project investigates an 11-year dataset of Gross Domestic Product (GDP) to uncover long-term macroeconomic trends, growth trajectories, and structural economic shifts. Utilizing Python's data science ecosystem—including Pandas and NumPy for rigorous data cleaning and time-series profiling, alongside Matplotlib and Seaborn for visualization—the analysis isolates definitive periods of market expansion, contraction, and volatility. By tracking year-over-year percentage variations and segmenting data by contributing sectors or regions, the project successfully identifies key economic drivers and historical anomalies, transforming raw historical figures into actionable economic insights.
